Exam technique

Pick up easy marks

  1. Show your working. Method marks are given even if the final answer is wrong.
  2. Read the accuracy. Give answers to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise (angles to 1 d.p.).
  3. Keep full values. Don't round in the middle of a calculation, only at the end.
  4. Check units. cm vs m, cm² vs cm³. Convert before you calculate.
  5. Calculator mode. Make sure it's in degrees, not radians.
  6. Use the marks as a guide. A 4-mark question needs about four steps.
